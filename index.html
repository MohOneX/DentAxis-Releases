FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does DentAxis need an internet connection?

No. The desktop app works fully offline and stores data on your PC. Internet is only used for optional updates, the store, and the cloud features included in Pro and Max.

Who owns my clinic data?

You do. On Free, data never leaves your clinic. On Pro and Max, a secure copy is backed up to the cloud under your own account only.

What is the difference between Pro and Max?

Both back up your clinic to the cloud. Pro lets you open the clinic portal on your phone and read everything. Max lets you also add and edit — patients, appointments, treatments and invoices — giving you full control from anywhere.

How do I upgrade to Pro or Max?

Message us on WhatsApp. We activate the subscription on your Google account, and cloud backup and the portal unlock immediately in the app.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 and 11, in both 64-bit and 32-bit editions. Updates are delivered automatically inside the app.

Ready to modernize your clinic?

Download DentAxis free today, or message us to activate Pro or Max — we usually reply within a few hours.
